# Doug MacDonald Memorial Transcript

A shareable record of the memorial for Norman Douglas (Doug) MacDonald, built from a phone recording of the service.

## Language

**Doug**:
Norman Douglas MacDonald, whose memorial this is. The transcript calls him Doug.
_Avoid_: Norman, Dr. MacDonald (except inside quoted speech)

**Recording**:
The original Voice Memos audio file of the service, kept unaltered.
_Avoid_: original, raw audio

**Cleaned audio**:
An edited copy of the Recording, processed so voices are easier to hear. It runs on the same timeline as the Recording.
_Avoid_: enhanced audio, remaster

**Apple transcript**:
The automatic text that Voice Memos made from the Recording. It is used only to cross-check.
_Avoid_: original transcript

**Transcript**:
The cleaned, clean-verbatim text of the service, with each Line linked to a time in the audio. Filler words are removed and misheard words are fixed, but each speaker's phrasing is kept.

**Speaker**:
A person who addressed the room during the service.

**Talk**:
One Speaker's continuous turn at the front of the room. One Speaker can give more than one Talk. For example, Sharon opens and closes the service, and Ray gives a tribute and also reads a poem.
_Avoid_: speech, segment, section

**Piping**:
The bagpipe performance that closes the service. It appears in the Transcript as an entry with no words. The Cleaned audio leaves it as it is, apart from volume leveling.

**Line**:
The smallest clickable unit of the Transcript. It has a start time, so selecting it plays the audio from that point.
_Avoid_: segment, caption, cue

## People

**Sharon**: Doug's wife. She hosts the service.
**Amy**: Sharon and Doug's daughter, and Ted's wife.
**Alex**: Sharon and Doug's son. He speaks first among the family.
**Ivan** and **Hazel**: Amy and Ted's children, Doug's grandchildren. Their names for Doug and Sharon are "Boppa" and "Neena."
**Joe Cosgrove**: Doug's friend from the Coast Guard reserve.
**Ray Anschel**: Doug's colleague at Normandale Community College. He gives a tribute and reads "Crossing the Bar."
**Mike Breidenbach**: The bagpiper who plays at the close, Director of Piping at Bagpipe Associates.
